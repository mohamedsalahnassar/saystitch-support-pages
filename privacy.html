Plain-language privacy

Privacy Policy

Saystitch has no account system or Saystitch-operated servers. Your dictation history and settings stay on your device.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Microphone audio and speech recognition

The microphone is active while you dictate and, if you leave the optional ready period on, while Saystitch is ready for another dictation. iOS shows its orange microphone indicator while the microphone is active. You can turn the ready period off in Settings or stop it immediately from Saystitch.

Saystitch uses Apple's on-device speech model when one is available for your language and device. If an on-device model cannot be used, the app may use Apple's speech recognition service, which can process audio outside your device. Apple’s privacy terms apply to Apple’s processing.

Writing styles

AI writing styles use Apple Intelligence on your device when Apple Intelligence is available and enabled. If it is unavailable, Saystitch uses basic local cleanup. Saystitch does not send text to its own servers for rewriting.

Data stored on your device

  • Saystitch stores your settings, selected writing style and personal dictionary locally.
  • The History tab stores your 100 most recent dictation results on this device. Use Clear in History to remove them.
  • The keyboard and app share dictation results and settings through their iOS App Group container. Saystitch uses Keyboard Full Access for this exchange.

Saystitch does not operate a service that collects this information. Local data may be included in device backups depending on your iOS backup settings.

Keyboard Full Access

iOS requires Full Access so the keyboard extension can communicate with its containing app through the shared App Group. Saystitch uses that access for dictation results, settings and keyboard setup status. The app has no Saystitch server and the keyboard does not send your typed text or dictation history to a Saystitch server. Apple speech recognition may process audio as described above.

Collection, ads and analytics

Saystitch does not have an account system, ads, analytics or a Saystitch-operated backend. We do not collect or sell your dictation history, personal dictionary or typed text.
