How Saystitch dictation works

  1. Add the keyboard. In Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards, add Saystitch and turn on Allow Full Access.
  2. Tap the mic. In any app, switch to Saystitch and tap the microphone. Speak naturally.
  3. Send polished text. Tap again to stop. Saystitch types clean text in the writing mode you picked.

Frequently asked questions

What is Saystitch?

Saystitch is a voice keyboard for iPhone and iPad. Tap the mic in any app, speak naturally, and Saystitch turns your speech into clean text in the style you choose.

Does Saystitch work in every app?

Yes. Because it's a keyboard, it works anywhere you can type, from Messages and WhatsApp to Mail, Notes, Slack and ChatGPT.

Is my voice private?

Saystitch has no account and no servers of its own. It uses on-device speech recognition and Apple Intelligence where your device supports them. Read the privacy policy.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Try every feature with free dictation minutes, then unlock Saystitch Pro once. There is no subscription.

Which languages does Saystitch support?

Saystitch dictates in the languages Apple's speech recognition supports on your device, and an optional advanced model adds many more, including Arabic.
